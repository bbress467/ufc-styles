UFC style map

Each dot is a UFC fighter with five or more UFC fights since 2000, placed by how they fight rather than how good they are. Left to right runs from more striking volume to more takedowns and control. Bottom to top runs from head strikes thrown at range to more work in the clinch and to the body. Each fighter is compared with others in the same division and era.

Hover over a dot for a quick look, click or tap it for the fighter's card, or search for a fighter.

    Styles are a continuum: fighters near the center have no strong lean either way. Positions are relative to fighters in the same division and era.

    Data: neelagiriaditya/ufc-datasets-1994-2025, a public Kaggle dataset (CC0) scraped from ufcstats.com, covering UFC fights from 2000 through August 2026.
